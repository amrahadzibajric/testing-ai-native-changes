## Description

Turns every open or Proposed decision that the build depends on into a recorded disposition. The build proceeds on the stated working assumptions; this feature makes them visible, owned and reversible at low cost.

## Scope Detail

- Client confirmation pack for ADR-0011 (CSV format and rejection), ADR-0012 (clean-up ownership), ADR-0013 (30-minute remainder minimum), and the ADR-0014 and ADR-0015 rules that only the project lead has confirmed
- Disposition of ADR-0018 (sweeper), ADR-0019 (time handling), ADR-0020 (concurrency) and ADR-0022 (current-user seam) by the project lead
- Build-start open items: exact IANA zone, bootstrap admin settings, behaviour at the 50-person cap, definition of "import settled"

## Technical Approach

Short decision documents and an outcome log. Answers are written back through the `create-adr` skill where an ADR status changes, and into the affected feature files where scope changes.
