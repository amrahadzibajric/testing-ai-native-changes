## Description

Configures automated backups and documents recovery so that data is restorable within the stated targets: RPO 24 hours, RTO one business day.

## Scope Detail

- Azure SQL automated backup and point-in-time restore retention
- Recovery runbook including environment recreation from Bicep

## Technical Approach

Platform backup settings in Bicep; a short runbook; rehearsal happens in F-17.01.

## Stories

---

### S-12.04.01: Configure backups to meet the recovery point

**Estimated Hours:** TBD

As a Software Engineer, I want backups configured so that no more than 24 hours of data can be lost.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- [ ] Point-in-time restore is enabled with retention that satisfies RPO 24 hours.
- [ ] The setting is defined in the Bicep templates.
- [ ] Encryption at rest is confirmed.

_Notes:_ NFR-008.

---

### S-12.04.02: Write the recovery runbook

**Estimated Hours:** TBD

As a Program Architect, I want a recovery runbook so that the team can restore service within one business day.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- [ ] The runbook covers restoring the database to a point in time and recreating the environment from Bicep.
- [ ] It states who performs each step and the expected duration against RTO one business day.
- [ ] It is reviewed and rehearsed in F-17.01.

_Notes:_ NFR-008.
