## Description

A short window after go-live in which the delivery team triages and fixes defects, watches monitoring and hands over.

## Scope Detail

- Reduced-hours engineering and QA presence for one week
- Monitoring review and follow-up list
- Handover

## Technical Approach

Triage through Application Insights and the audit trail; defects fixed and verified through the pipeline.

## Stories

---

### S-17.03.01: Triage and fix defects during the first week

**Estimated Hours:** TBD

As a Software Engineer, I want a triage routine so that live defects are fixed and verified quickly.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- [ ] Defects reported by admins or users are logged, prioritised and fixed through the normal pipeline.
- [ ] Each fix is verified by a QA engineer before release.
- [ ] Data corrections, if any, are made through the application and audited.

_Notes:_ Hypercare staffing includes at least one engineer and one QA at reduced hours.

---

### S-17.03.02: Review monitoring and hand over

**Estimated Hours:** TBD

As a Program Architect, I want a closing review so that the pilot passes to steady state with known follow-ups.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- [ ] Telemetry and audit trail are reviewed for errors, sweeper delays and unexpected admin actions.
- [ ] A closing note lists open items and follow-up work, including the Entra sign-in phase (E-16) and any scale-up questions.
- [ ] Handover is acknowledged by the project lead.

_Notes:_ NFR-006, NFR-019.
